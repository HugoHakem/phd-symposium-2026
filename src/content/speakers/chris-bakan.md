Chris Bakan is Senior Vice President and Chief Science Engineering Officer of Roche Genentech R&D's Autonomous Lab Accelerator in South San Francisco. The Accelerator brings together Roche and Genentech research, technical operations and computational sciences to build closed-loop "lab-in-the-loop" capabilities, in which AI designs experiments and automated laboratories run them.

Previously, as Global Head of Computational Science & Informatics R&D at Roche, she led a global organisation responsible for computational biology and bioinformatics, AI and machine-learning algorithms, and data science, as well as software-as-a-medical-device R&D for genomic, digital pathology and biomarker-based diagnostics.

Chris joined Roche Sequencing Solutions as Head of Software & Bioinformatics from Cisco, where she was Senior Director of Core Software Technologies and Software-Defined Networking. Before that she held senior roles in enterprise software architecture, engineering and product management at BMC Software and Oracle, where she holds four patents. She holds degrees in Biology and Neuroscience from UC Berkeley and is a graduate of the Stanford Graduate School of Business.
