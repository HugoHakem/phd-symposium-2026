Paul Flicek is a Research Professor at the Institute for Systems Genomics of the University of Connecticut, where he started in 2026. He is an EMBO Member.

Following a BS in Physics from Drake University, Paul earned a Doctor of Science in Biomedical Engineering from Washington University in St Louis where he participated in the initial sequencing of the human and mouse genomes. He moved to Cambridge, England, in 2005 for a postdoc at the European Bioinformatics Institute (EMBL-EBI), where he became a member of the EMBL faculty in 2007 and held progressively senior roles including Associate Director for EMBL-EBI Services starting in 2019. His team at EBI developed and maintained Ensembl, a genome browser and annotation resource that serves tens of thousands of users daily. Paul became the inaugural Chief Data Science Officer at The Jackson Laboratory in 2023, where he stayed until moving to UConn in 2026.

Throughout his career, which has been characterised by projects on genome annotation, comparative regulatory genomics, and large-scale biological data, he has been involved with many major international collaborative efforts including ENCODE, the 1000 Genomes Project, and the International Human Epigenome Consortium.
