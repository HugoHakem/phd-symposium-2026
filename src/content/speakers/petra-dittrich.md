Petra Dittrich is Professor for Bioanalytics at the Department of Biosystems Science and Engineering at ETH Zurich. She studied chemistry at the Universities of Bielefeld and Salamanca and obtained her PhD in 2003 at the Max Planck Institute for Biophysical Chemistry. Following postdoctoral research at the Leibniz Institute for Analytical Sciences in Dortmund, she conducted research stays at Cornell University and the University of Tokyo. Since joining ETH Zurich in 2008, she has established an internationally recognised research program in bioanalytics and microfluidics. Her work has been recognised with numerous distinctions, including European Research Council Starting and Consolidator Grants, the Analytica Research Prize of the Society of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, and the Heinrich Emanuel Merck Prize.

Her research focuses on the development of microfluidic and lab-on-chip technologies for applications in the life sciences, integrating chemistry, engineering, physics, and biology in highly interdisciplinary ways. Her group develops miniaturised analytical platforms for single-cell analysis, droplet microfluidics, artificial cells, membrane studies, and diagnostic applications. By combining automation and high-throughput bioanalytical methodologies, her work enables precise investigation of complex biological systems and contributes to the advancement of next-generation tools for diagnostics, drug discovery, and cellular analysis.
