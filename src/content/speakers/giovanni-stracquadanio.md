Giovanni Stracquadanio is a Personal Chair in Engineering Biology at the University of Edinburgh, where he also serves as a UKRI EPSRC Fellow, Deputy Director of the Centre for Engineering Biology, and Co-director of the Edinburgh Genome Foundry. With over 15 years of experience intersecting artificial intelligence, computational biology, and molecular genetics, his research focuses on using generative AI and advanced automation to address major biomedical challenges.

He was a key contributor to the international Synthetic Yeast Genome Project (Sc2.0), pioneering the core algorithms and software used to design the world's first synthetic eukaryotic genome. His current work bridges the gap between machine learning and medicine, leveraging lab automation to engineer next-generation enzyme replacement therapies for rare genetic conditions.
