Robert Waterhouse is the director of the Environmental Bioinformatics Group at the SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics. The group's activities and research are focused on developing data science solutions to address the challenge of protecting and restoring the environment that sustains humankind. By meeting key needs in biodiversity data collection, integration, and analysis, the team delivers tools and services to support data-driven, empirically informed, and model-guided policies and actions.
