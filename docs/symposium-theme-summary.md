# Shifting Gears: Automation, AI, and High-Throughput Methodologies

Automation, scalable experimental design, high-throughput methodologies, and artificial intelligence are accelerating the pace of scientific discovery while enhancing reliability and reproducibility. These technological advances are profoundly impacting the scientific community, bridging the gap between fundamental biological questions, theoretical development, and biomedical breakthroughs.

The 2026 EMBL PhD Symposium aims to explore the latest technological advancements in automation, high-throughput methodologies, and scalable data analysis across diverse disciplines. Our goal is to highlight the wide-ranging applications of these tools, from foundational academic research to industry-scale implementation.

## Speaker profiles and research themes

We are looking to assemble an interdisciplinary group of scientists and engineers to share how they are scaling up discovery using cutting-edge technologies and methodologies. We are seeking speakers whose research aligns with the following themes:

* **Automation and robotics:** Scaling experiments while maintaining precision, reproducibility, and reliability.  
* **High-throughput everything:** From large-scale screening to systems biology.

* **From prototype to platform:** Biomedical engineering and the translational application of biological discoveries.

* **Telemetry to theory:** Leveraging real-time data for real-time decision-making and model building.  
* **Fast-tracking analysis:** Harnessing AI and machine learning for data analysis at scale.

These themes serve as broad guidelines rather than strict boundaries. While our primary objective is to feature speakers who develop or utilize these technologies, it is equally important to us that the symposium covers a diverse range of fundamental and applied biological questions.

Based on the success of last year's symposium, attendees valued the insights shared by former EMBL PhD students and researchers. Hearing about the diverse career trajectories and research endeavors of EMBL alumni provided a great perspective on the wide range of opportunities available after EMBL. To build on this, we are particularly keen to invite EMBL alumni to share their current research and professional experiences within this fast-developing field, showcasing diverse pathways in both academia and industry.
